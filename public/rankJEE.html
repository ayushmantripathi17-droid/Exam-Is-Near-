FAQ

Common questions

How accurate is this JEE percentile predictor?
Estimates are based on NTA's official score-percentile data from JEE Main 2025. Actual percentile varies session-to-session due to normalisation — use this as a reliable reference range.
What percentile do I need for NIT Trichy CSE?
NIT Trichy CSE typically closes around 99.1–99.3 percentile for general category under the home state quota. For other state quota it is slightly higher. Top branches at premier NITs require 98.5+ percentile.
Does JEE Main have negative marking?
Yes — MCQs follow +4 for correct and −1 for incorrect answers. Numerical answer type questions carry +4 for correct and no penalty for wrong answers.
What is the difference between JEE Main percentile and rank?
Percentile is your relative score — it shows what percentage of candidates scored below you. Rank is derived from percentile across all sessions. A 99 percentile roughly corresponds to rank ~11,000 given ~11 lakh candidates.
