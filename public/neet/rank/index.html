FAQ

Common questions

How accurate is this rank predictor?
The estimates are based on official NTA rank-marks data from NEET 2025. Actual ranks vary slightly year-on-year based on difficulty and total candidates, so use this as a reference range.
What is a good NEET score for a government MBBS seat?
Generally, 550+ marks gives you a realistic shot at a government MBBS seat under the All India Quota. For top AIIMS / JIPMER, you need 680+.
When does NEET 2026 rank list come out?
NTA typically releases the NEET result and rank list within 3–4 weeks of the exam. Counselling through MCC starts 4–6 weeks after results.
Does negative marking affect my score?
Yes — NEET follows +4 for correct and −1 for incorrect answers. Unattempted questions carry no penalty. Calculate your score carefully before entering it above.
