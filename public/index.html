Exam Is Near — Free AI-Powered Study App for JEE, NEET & UPSC by ArkSetu

Exam Is Near is a free AI-powered study companion built by ArkSetu for students preparing for JEE Mains, JEE Advanced, NEET UG, UPSC, CBSE Class 10, CBSE Class 12, and NFSU exams. Study smarter with our all-in-one Progressive Web App — no download required, works offline.

Features

  • Pomodoro study timer with session tracking and custom alarms
  • AI Tutor powered by Groq AI for instant doubt solving
  • Flashcards with spaced repetition (SRS) for long-term retention
  • Quiz mode for self-testing with instant feedback
  • Syllabus tracker with chapter-wise progress analytics
  • JEE rank predictor and NEET rank predictor from marks
  • Chapter-wise weightage analysis for JEE and NEET
  • OMR sheet practice for NEET and JEE exam simulation
  • Study material file manager with Google Drive sync
  • Mood and energy log with daily study analytics
  • Works offline as a PWA — installable on Android and iOS
  • Free to use — no sign-up required to start

Who is it for?

Exam Is Near is designed for JEE aspirants, NEET aspirants, UPSC candidates, CBSE students (Class 10 and Class 12), and NFSU students across India. Whether you are preparing for board exams or competitive entrance exams, this free study app helps you stay on track.

About ArkSetu

ArkSetu is an Indian ed-tech developer building free, accessible tools for students. Exam Is Near is hosted at exam-is-near.web.app and is completely free to use.
