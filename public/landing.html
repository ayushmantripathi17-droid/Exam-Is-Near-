Common questions

Is Exam Is Near really free?
Yes! The core features — Pomodoro timer, syllabus tracker, basic flashcards — are completely free with no time limit. Pro is optional and unlocks AI features and removes ads.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
Sign in with Google, go to Pro → Start Free Trial. No payment details needed. After 7 days, you return to the free plan automatically. One trial per account.
Can I use it offline?
Yes! Exam Is Near is a PWA (Progressive Web App). Install it from your browser and most features work offline. AI features require internet.
Which payment methods are supported?
UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, and NetBanking — all via Razorpay, India's most trusted payment gateway.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription?
Yes, cancel any time from within the app. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period. No hidden fees.
Is my study data safe?
Pro user data is backed up to Firebase (Google Cloud) and only accessible to you when signed in. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
