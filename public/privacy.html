Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  ArkSetu / Exam Is Near  ·  exam-is-near.web.app

We built Exam Is Near to help students study smarter. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains clearly what data we collect, why, and how we protect it. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

1. Who We Are

Exam Is Near is a free AI-powered study companion developed by ArkSetu. You can reach us at the contact details at the bottom of this page. References to "we", "us", or "our" in this policy mean ArkSetu.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google OAuth.
  • Study data — Your syllabus progress, flashcards, quiz results, Pomodoro sessions, study logs, and analytics you generate inside the app.
  • Uploaded files — Notes, PDFs, and images you upload to the Files section. These are stored locally on your device (free plan) or in Firebase Cloud Storage (Pro plan).
  • Payment information — If you subscribe to Pro, payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card or UPI details. We only receive a payment confirmation token.
  • Usage data — Basic analytics such as pages visited, feature usage, and session duration, collected via Google Analytics to help us improve the app.
  • Device data — Browser type, operating system, and screen size for PWA compatibility purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Exam Is Near study features
  • To sync your study data across devices (Pro plan via Firebase)
  • To process your Pro subscription payment via Razorpay
  • To send you important account or service notifications (not marketing spam)
  • To understand how users interact with the app and improve the experience
  • To serve relevant advertisements to free-plan users via Google AdSense

4. Google AdSense & Advertising

Free-plan users may see advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and device identifiers to show relevant ads based on your browsing behaviour. We do not control which ads are shown.

You can manage your ad preferences at adssettings.google.com or opt out of personalised ads. Pro plan subscribers see no advertisements.

5. AI Assistant & Data Processing

The AI Tutor feature sends your questions to an AI language model API for processing. We do not permanently store your AI chat messages on our servers beyond the current session (free plan). Pro plan users' chat history is stored encrypted in Firebase and accessible only to you.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and Cloud Storage
  • Razorpay — Payment processing for Pro subscriptions
  • Google AdSense — Advertising for free-plan users
  • Google Analytics — App usage analytics
  • Google Fonts — Typography (Inter, JetBrains Mono)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your study data on the free plan is stored locally in your browser (localStorage / IndexedDB) and never leaves your device unless you enable Sync. Pro plan data is stored in Firebase Cloud (Google infrastructure) with industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile section of the app. Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Exam Is Near is intended for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing
  • Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

11. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication (keeping you signed in), preferences, and analytics. Google AdSense also uses cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the app may not work correctly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify users via an in-app announcement banner. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
